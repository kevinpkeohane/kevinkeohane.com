01 · Decide

Positioning & strategic focus

Decide who you’re built to win and why they should choose you over the default option.

What’s presented

“We need a rebrand. Buyers keep comparing us on price.”

What’s actually happening

You’re competing on the category’s default checklist, where everyone sounds the same.

A fresh look won’t fix an unsettled position. You win by changing what buyers think matters before procurement puts you side by side with the competition.

What’s presented

“We serve lots of industries — we need messaging that keeps all our options open.”

What’s actually happening

Trying to speak to everyone dilutes the story until no one feels spoken to.

Growth stalls when leadership avoids trade-offs. Clear positioning names who you’re best for, what you stand for, and what you’re willing to leave behind.

How I help: I work with leadership to lock down the target buyer, define a space you can credibly own, and back it with proof that holds up in a real buying conversation.

In practice: EarthScope ($400M+ contract won and defended) · LECG (Red Dot Award brand refresh & values)

02 · Build

Narrative, GTM & commercial engine

Turn your position into one story, proof and GTM system that moves deals forward.

What’s presented

“We spend on marketing, stay busy, and still can’t see pipeline move.”

What’s actually happening

Marketing and sales are telling different stories — or optimising hard against a message that never settled.

The real cost isn’t wasted ad spend. It’s eighteen months spent executing the wrong story competently while sales improvises in every room.

What’s presented

“We’ve grown on referrals and relationships, but outbound and campaigns aren’t landing.”

What’s actually happening

The story still lives in the founder’s head.

What closes in a warm introduction rarely translates to cold buyers until your point of view is turned into an offer, proof and sales tools anyone on the team can carry.

How I help: I translate the position into one usable narrative, offer proof and a go-to-market operating system connecting campaigns, partners and sales around the same buyers.

In practice: Deloitte (40+ go-to-market & sales-enablement playbooks, $10M–$10B+ targets) · Go-to-Market Maven™

03 · Align

Culture, employee experience & delivery

Make sure the business can actually deliver what the brand promises.

What’s presented

“We agreed the strategy in the room. Six months later, nothing changed.”

What’s actually happening

Adoption is the deliverable, not the deck.

A strategy written centrally without the people expected to carry it gets nodded through in leadership meetings and quietly ignored in day-to-day work.

What’s presented

“Turnover is up, customer scores are slipping, and good people are harder to hire.”

What’s actually happening

Your external promise has outrun operational reality — and employees are absorbing the gap.

That isn’t an HR problem; it’s a commercial leak you pay for twice, in attrition and in churn.

How I help: Using EX2CX, I trace where employee-experience friction breaks the customer promise, then align leadership priorities, behaviors, employer brand and frontline routines so delivery matches the story.

In practice: EY (one story & global EVP across 150 countries, 300,000 people) · Securian Canada · BT Openreach (“Right First Time”)

04 · Grow

New markets, architecture & repeatable systems

Make what works travel — into new markets, across offerings, and through repeatable workflows.

What’s presented

“We’re a smaller business that’s saturated our own region and can’t seem to break into new markets.”

What’s actually happening

Local reputation doesn’t cross borders on its own.

What won you work where people already know you hasn’t been packaged into a clear proposition, proof and repeatable go-to-market playbook for buyers who’ve never heard your name.

What’s presented

“We’ve grown into several companies sharing a logo.”

What’s actually happening

Growth or acquisitions exposed decisions no one owns — what stays consistent at the top, what flexes underneath, and who decides.

Layering on more campaigns or AI before settling that architecture only multiplies the noise instead of scaling what works.

How I help: Whether you’re taking a proven regional business into new markets or bringing coherence across products, locations or acquisitions, I build the brand architecture, repeatable GTM workflows and AI-enabled standards that let good work travel without reinventing it every time.

In practice: SimonMed (brand transformation & architecture across 150+ US locations) · LTIMindtree (five AI offerings, one idea) · Dormakaba

These aren’t four separate projects. A regional business breaking into new markets often discovers a Decide or Build constraint first — and a sharper story won’t hold if your people can’t deliver it. I start where growth is stuck and follow the problem across teams.
